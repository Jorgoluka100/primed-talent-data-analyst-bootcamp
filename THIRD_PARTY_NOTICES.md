# Third-Party Data and Teaching Materials

The repository's MIT licence applies to original code and documentation authored for this portfolio. It does **not** relicense included teaching datasets, course resources or other third-party materials.

Datasets such as Sample Superstore, Ames Housing and Uber pickup data remain subject to the terms and attribution requirements of their original sources. Verify those terms before redistribution or commercial use.

The public `main` branch is curated as a portfolio. Historical classroom/instructor material is preserved in repository history/archive branches rather than presented as original portfolio authorship.
